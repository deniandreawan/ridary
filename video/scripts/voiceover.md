# Ridary demo · voiceover guide

Record dry, close, and calm. WAV or M4A is fine; do not add music or effects.
The final cut is 162 seconds. Read at a measured pace and
leave the natural pauses at the end of each section.

## INTRO · 00:00–00:12

Your ride knows where you were, how you moved, and what almost happened. Ridary keeps that private, then uses hybrid AI through Firebase AI Logic to turn it into proof.

## PROBLEM · 00:12–00:28

Most safety apps upload the ride first. Ridary flips the trust boundary: hybrid AI starts on the phone. Raw GPS, motion, and incident windows stay on the iPhone. Only a derived summary can leave.

## RECORD · 00:28–00:50

Start a ride, then put the phone away. No camera, no audio. GPS and motion are captured locally. The last 30 seconds can be locked on device, and nothing is uploaded. ## ONDEVICE · 00:50–01:06

Back on screen, the recap is generated with Apple Foundation Models. The badge reads On device because the sensitive reasoning stays here. If the model is unavailable, local rules take over — not the cloud.

## ASK · 01:06–01:20

Ask Ridary uses the same boundary. It answers from a compact local context, and labels every answer: On device, In cloud, or Rules.

## CLOUD · 01:20–01:44

The cloud gets a different job. Anonymized events become a community risk map. Riders compare their week, risk cells, and the moments that need attention. Patterns, not raw routes. Collective insight without surveillance.

## PROOF · 01:44–01:58

Then a signed safety band. An insurer verifies the score, the period, and the Ed25519 signature. They see proof, not your route, your sensors, or your identity.

## TRUST · 01:58–02:20

This is the hybrid split. Private reasoning at the edge. Collective insight in the cloud. Raw GPS and sensor windows never cross this line. Only a derived summary and a signature do. Firebase App Check protects every call, and the same data contracts are ready for Android next.

## IMPACT · 02:20–02:33

Ridary gives riders a voice, cities a signal, and insurers a proof. No camera. No audio. No automatic dispatch. Just a private witness.

## OUTRO · 02:33–02:42

Every ride. On record. Ridary. Private by design. Proof on demand.
